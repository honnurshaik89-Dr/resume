Professional Summary

Bridging academia and industry

Chartered Engineer (CEng, UK) and PhD-qualified academic with 12 years of combined academic and industry experience across Oman, the UK and India — spanning photovoltaic system degradation and performance modelling, AI/ML-driven solar and wind forecasting, offshore and floating PV, and IoT-based energy monitoring.

Currently Assistant Professor in Renewable Energy at Muscat University, where I serve as Programme Coordinator and Chair of the Curriculum Review Committee for BEng Energy Engineering, and as External Examiner at Sultan Qaboos University and the University of Buraimi. Principal or Co-Investigator on five competitive grants totalling USD 134,000, and a featured speaker at World Engineering Day 2026 (UNESCO / WFEO).

Author of 38 peer-reviewed outputs (h-index 12, i10-index 14, 620+ citations) in Q1/Q2 venues including Results in Engineering (IF 9.4), Alexandria Engineering Journal (IF 6.2) and Energies (IF 3.9). Proven record in programme leadership, ABET and OAAA accreditation, grant and journal reviewing, and supervision of 25+ postgraduate and undergraduate projects. Hands-on industry background delivering technical projects worth more than USD 300,000.
