Professional Summary

Chartered Engineer & Academic Researcher

Chartered Engineer (UK) and PhD-qualified academic with 10+ years of combined university teaching, applied research, and industry leadership spanning four complementary domains: renewable energy and photovoltaic systems, AI/ML-based solar and wind forecasting, wireless communications and antenna design, and the Internet of Medical Things (IoMT).

Currently serving as Assistant Professor in Renewable Energy at Muscat University, and Principal Investigator on a Research Council of Oman (TRC) grant of ~USD 50,000 for deep-learning-based solar forecasting. Also PI on a recently submitted ~USD 14,000 Muscat University internal seed grant for an AI-enabled smart micro weather station for solar forecasting and green-hydrogen estimation.

Author of 36 peer-reviewed publications (h-index 12, i10-index 14, 600+ citations) including multiple Q1/Q2 journals. Proven record in programme leadership, ABET/OAAA accreditation, research grant reviewing, and supervision of postgraduate and undergraduate research. Hands-on industry background in technical project delivery worth more than USD 300,000.
