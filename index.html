Renewable Energy & PV Systems

Field-based PV degradation analysis, polysilicon and thin-film module performance under Gulf climatic conditions, offshore floating PV, hybrid wind–solar systems, and life-cycle assessment of renewables.

AI / Machine Learning for Energy Forecasting

Deep Learning · Smart Grids
