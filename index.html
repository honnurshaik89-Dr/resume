Areas of Expertise

Research Focus

Active research and publication across three interlinked engineering domains.

☀️

Renewable Energy & PV Systems

Field-based PV degradation analysis, polysilicon and thin-film module performance under Gulf climatic conditions, offshore floating PV, hybrid wind-solar systems, and life-cycle assessment of renewables.

🌞 Solar • Wind • Green Hydrogen
🤖

AI / Machine Learning for Energy Forecasting

Deep-learning, federated learning (GRU), SARIMAX, Prophet, NARMAX and wavelet-transform models for solar irradiance and wind power forecasting in smart-grid networks.

🧠 Deep Learning • Smart Grids
📱

IoT and Connected Systems for Smart Energy

IoT architectures for smart-grid monitoring and renewable energy management; wireless sensor networks for real-time PV performance tracking; AI-driven IoT applications in energy forecasting and predictive maintenance.

🔌 IoT • Smart Grids • Connected Systems
