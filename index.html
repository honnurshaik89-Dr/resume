Areas of Expertise

Research Focus

Active research and publication across four interlinked engineering domains.

☀️

Renewable Energy & PV Systems

Field-based PV degradation analysis, polysilicon and thin-film module performance under Gulf climatic conditions, offshore floating PV, hybrid wind-solar systems, and life-cycle assessment of renewables.

🌞 Solar • Wind • Green Hydrogen
🤖

AI / Machine Learning for Energy Forecasting

Deep-learning, federated learning (GRU), SARIMAX, Prophet, NARMAX and wavelet-transform models for solar irradiance and wind power forecasting in smart-grid networks.

🧠 Deep Learning • Smart Grids
📡

Wireless Communications & Antenna Systems

Millimetre-wave propagation, 60 GHz conical-horn antennas, beam-steering linear arrays for small-cell 5G mobile networks, and mm-wave forest-propagation measurements.

🔌 5G • mm-Wave • Antenna Design
🏥️

Internet of Medical Things (IoMT)

Opportunities, challenges and architectures for IoMT, and imperceptible robust watermarking for secure medical-image transmission in cyber-secured smart cities.

🔒 IoMT • Cybersecurity • Healthcare
