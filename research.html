Domains

Research Areas

Renewable Energy & Photovoltaic Systems

Domain 1

Field-based and simulation-driven research on photovoltaic degradation, module performance under the Gulf's harsh climate, and emerging solar technologies including floating PV and hybrid systems.

  • Field-validated PV output-power degradation (1.96%/year benchmark for Oman — ~2× European rates)
  • Polysilicon and thin-film module performance under Gulf climatic conditions
  • Offshore and floating photovoltaic system feasibility in the Persian Gulf
  • Hybrid wind-solar energy system design and feasibility studies
  • Battery energy storage and PV/battery energy management systems
  • Green-hydrogen desalination and life-cycle assessment of renewables
  • 500 MW Manah IPP utility-scale solar plant analysis (Oman)

AI / Machine Learning for Energy Forecasting

Domain 2

Advanced AI and deep-learning models for accurate solar irradiance, solar-power and wind-power forecasting in smart-grid environments.

  • Deep-learning and federated-learning (GRU) models for solar irradiance forecasting
  • SARIMAX-based machine learning for long-term solar forecasting (Muscat, Oman)
  • Prophet-based ML framework for solar energy potential estimation
  • NARMAX and wavelet-transform hybrid models for wind power forecasting
  • Thermal-management modelling of lithium-ion batteries
  • Soft-computing control of DC-DC converters (Herd Immunity Optimisation)
  • 4H-SiC MESFET parameter identification using Modified Grey Wolf Optimisation

IoT & Connected Systems for Smart Energy

Domain 3

IoT architectures and connected systems for smart-grid monitoring, renewable energy management, and AI-driven forecasting, informed by a background in wireless communications.

  • IoT architectures for smart-grid monitoring and renewable energy management
  • Wireless sensor networks for real-time PV performance tracking and fault detection
  • Secure data communication protocols for smart energy infrastructure
  • AI-driven IoT applications in energy forecasting and predictive maintenance
  • AI-driven IoT applications in predictive maintenance for renewable assets

Wireless Communications & Antennas

Domain 4

Millimetre-wave antenna design and propagation modelling, underpinning connected-energy and 5G sensing systems.

  • 60 GHz millimetre-wave antenna design for short-range, high-data-rate links
  • Indoor/outdoor link-budget analysis and propagation measurements
  • MIMO systems and mm-wave sensing for wireless sensor networks
  • DRDO-funded mm-wave antenna research (INR 36 lakh / ~USD 43,000)
