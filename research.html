Domains

Research Areas

Each domain is supported by peer-reviewed publications, funded research projects, and real-world field measurements.

☀️

Renewable Energy & Photovoltaic Systems

Domain 1

Field-based and simulation-driven research on photovoltaic degradation, module performance under the Gulf's harsh climatic conditions, and emerging solar technologies including floating PV and hybrid systems.

  • Field-validated PV output-power degradation (1.96%/year benchmark for Oman – ~2× European rates)
  • Polysilicon and thin-film module performance under Gulf climatic conditions
  • Offshore and floating photovoltaic system feasibility in the Persian Gulf
  • Hybrid wind-solar energy system design and feasibility studies
  • Battery energy storage and PV/battery energy management systems
  • Green-hydrogen desalination and life-cycle assessment of renewables
  • Smart-grid networks and energy management systems
  • 500 MW Manah IPP utility-scale solar plant analysis (Oman)
🤖

AI / Machine Learning for Energy Forecasting

Domain 2

Development and application of advanced AI and deep learning models for accurate solar irradiance, solar power, and wind power forecasting in smart-grid environments.

  • Deep-learning and federated-learning (GRU) models for solar irradiance forecasting
  • SARIMAX-based machine learning for long-term solar forecasting (Muscat, Oman)
  • Prophet-based ML framework for solar energy potential estimation
  • NARMAX and wavelet-transform hybrid models for wind power forecasting
  • Thermal-management modelling of lithium-ion batteries
  • Soft-computing control of DC-DC converters (Herd Immunity Optimisation)
  • 4H-SiC MESFET parameter identification using Modified Grey Wolf Optimisation
  • Novel Dynamic Temporal Interaction and Feature Synthesis framework
📡

Wireless Communications & Antenna Systems

Domain 3

Millimetre-wave and 5G communications research focusing on antenna design, propagation measurements, and small-cell network performance.

  • Millimetre-wave propagation measurements (indoor/outdoor environments)
  • 60 GHz concave-lens-integrated conical-horn antenna design and characterisation
  • Beam-steering linear array antennas for small-cell 5G mobile networks
  • mm-Wave forest-propagation measurements for 5G wireless sensor communications
  • Performance evaluation of mobile broadband cellular networks in populated cities
  • 4H-SiC MESFET parameter identification with surface and substrate trapping effects
  • DRDO-funded research on mm-Wave frequency antennas (INR 36 lakh / ~USD 43,000)
🏥️

Internet of Medical Things (IoMT) & Secure Healthcare

Domain 4

Opportunities, challenges, and security architectures for IoMT ecosystems, with a focus on cyber-secured data transmission and medical image protection.

  • IoMT opportunities, challenges, and architecture frameworks
  • Imperceptible robust watermarking for secure medical-image transmission
  • Ensuring integrity and security of medical images in IoMT environments
  • Cyber-secured smart cities powered by 100% renewables (analytical study)
  • IoMT in the era of COVID-19 (SEED-funded Principal Investigator)
