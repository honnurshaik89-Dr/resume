"Theory – Simulation – Practice" Learning Sequence

2026 · Pedagogy

A structured sequence integrating classroom fundamentals of solar PV, PVsyst-based system sizing and yield-estimation simulation, culminating in a solar-plant field trip for real-world exposure to plant layout, inverters, mounting structures and operational practices.

Rooftop PV Placement Programme — Muscat University

2025 · Hands-on

Supervised a rooftop PV placement programme providing hands-on student experiments on optimal tilt angles, shading-loss analysis, and PV power-output variations under Oman outdoor conditions.
