Outreach

Industry Engagement & Student Field Exposure

Bridging classroom learning with real-world renewable energy practice in Oman.

🏭
2026 • FIELD TRIP

500 MW Manah IPP Utility-Scale Solar Plant Visit

Organised and led an industry visit to the 500 MW Manah IPP utility-scale solar power plant in Oman for MSc Renewable Energy and BEng Energy Engineering students. Covered single-axis tracking design, over one million bifacial PV modules across 7.8 km², more than 1,800 inverters, and direct integration with the 400 kV national transmission grid.

📚
2026 • PEDAGOGY

"Theory – Simulation – Practice" Learning Sequence

Designed and delivered a structured learning sequence for Energy Engineering students integrating classroom fundamentals of solar PV systems, PVsyst-based system sizing and yield-estimation simulation, and culminating in a solar-plant field trip for real-world exposure to plant layout, inverters, mounting structures and operational practices.

🏠
2025 • HANDS-ON

Rooftop PV Placement Programme – Muscat University

Supervised a rooftop photovoltaic placement programme at Muscat University providing hands-on student experiments on optimal tilt angles, shading-loss analysis, and PV power-output variations under Oman outdoor conditions.

👥
ONGOING • ENGAGEMENT

Industry Guest Lectures & Practitioner Sessions

Organised industry guest lectures and practitioner sessions connecting students with engineers and managers from Oman's energy, utilities, and technology sectors at both A'Sharqiyah University and Muscat University.
